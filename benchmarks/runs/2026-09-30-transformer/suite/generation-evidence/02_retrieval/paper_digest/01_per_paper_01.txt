## Condition monitoring, diagnostics, and degradation indicators

- The paper combines two offline diagnostics for oil-immersed power transformers (PTs):
  - **SFRA** covers mechanical condition: windings, core and tap changer.
  - **DGA** covers electrical and thermal condition: the insulating oil and paper.
- **SFRA as a fingerprint:** the frequency-response transfer function (amplitude in dB, phase angle) is compared against a baseline or between phases. Deviations indicate winding or magnetic-circuit abnormalities.
- **SFRA quantitative indicator:** the correlation factor Rxy is computed in three bands:
  - RLF: 1–100 kHz
  - RMF: 100–600 kHz
  - RHF: 600 kHz–1 MHz
- **SFRA severity grading:**
  - Normal: RLF ≥ 2.0, RMF ≥ 1.0 and RHF ≥ 0.6
  - Slight deformation: 1.0 ≤ RLF < 2.0, or 0.6 ≤ RMF < 1.0
  - Obvious deformation: 0.6 ≤ RLF < 1.0, or RMF < 0.6
  - Severe deformation: RLF < 0.6
- **Case, Phan Thiet 63 MVA, 110/22 kV, YNyn0d11:**
  - Phase-pair RLF was 1.02–1.38, RMF 1.55–1.92 and RHF 1.08–1.36.
  - Diagnosis: slight deformation.
  - Note: the abstract lists this unit as 115/23/11 kV.
- **Case, Ngai Giao 40 MVA, 110/22 kV:**
  - RLF was 2.01–2.3, RMF 2.25–2.6 and RHF 1.85–2.52.
  - Diagnosis: normal, matching EVNSPC.
- **Other SFRA indicators noted from prior work:**
  - A negative capacitance change from a peak-frequency shift indicates insulation deterioration.
  - Tap changer coking shows as a low-frequency magnitude drop at 20 Hz–2 kHz.
  - Tap changer pitting shows as a shift to higher frequencies.
- **DGA indicators:**
  - Individual gas concentrations compared against thresholds.
  - Total combustible gas (TCG).
  - Rogers / IEC ratios: CH₄/H₂, C₂H₂/C₂H₄ and C₂H₄/C₂H₆.
  - Key-gas method using C₂H₄, CO, H₂ and C₂H₂.
- **DGA case, T1 20 MVA, 110/22 kV:**
  - H₂ 0, CH₄ 40.43, C₂H₆ 18.24, C₂H₄ 20.92, C₂H₂ 0 ppm.
  - CO₂ 8932.66, CO 2131.18 ppm (Table 12 lists CO as 2031.18).
  - O₂ 7805.28, N₂ 53315.2 ppm; TCG 2118 ppm.
  - Diagnosis: thermal fault below 300 °C (T1), matching EVNSPC.
  - CO is well above the 350 ppm IEC threshold.
- **MATLAB decision-support tools:**
  - SFRA-TRAFADI takes RLF, RMF and RHF as inputs.
  - DGA-TRAFADI takes H₂, CH₄, C₂H₆, C₂H₄, C₂H₂, CO₂ and CO as inputs and uses the Rogers ratio method.

## Maintenance practices, scheduling, and work-order–relevant context

- Check mechanical integrity after transportation, periodically, and after short circuits.
- Perform SFRA in these situations:
  - On every new PT, to capture the baseline fingerprint.
  - As part of routine electrical tests.
  - After reinstallation.
  - After long-term downstream short circuits.
  - After tap changer repair.
  - After vacuum treatment, oil filtration or oil regeneration.
  - After any fault in the PT.
- SFRA is commonly applied to transformers above 6 kV.
- Oil samples are analyzed by a testing center (the Southern Vietnam Power Testing Company, EVNSPC) to support timely maintenance decisions.
- Undetected winding deformation or core displacement can lead to costly repair or replacement. This motivates early diagnosis.

## Sensor modalities, measurements, and signal interpretation

- **SFRA test setup:**
  - Injects a low-voltage sinusoid of 1–10 Vrms.
  - Sweeps typically 20 Hz–2 MHz, depending on coil structure and the measurement system.
  - Amplitude = 20·log10(|Vmeas|/|Vref|); phase = phase(Vmeas) − phase(Vref).
- **Four SFRA measurement schemes:**
  - End-to-end open-circuit (EEOC)
  - End-to-end short-circuit (EESC)
  - Capacitive inter-winding (CAP)
  - Inductive inter-winding (IND)
- **Rxy computation:**
  - Compute the variances Dx and Dy, and the covariance Cxy.
  - LRxy = Cxy/√(DxDy).
  - Rxy = 10 if 1 − LRxy < 10⁻¹⁰; otherwise Rxy = −lg(1 − LRxy).
  - Comparisons are made phase-to-phase, e.g. A-N vs B-N with the other windings open.
- **Qualitative SFRA signatures** are illustrated for:
  - LV winding buckling
  - Twisting and loosened clamping
  - Axial deformation
  - Inter-turn short circuit
- **DGA measurement:**
  - Instrument: Agilent 7890A gas chromatograph, per ASTM D3612C, with a 1 ppm detection limit.
  - Gases measured: O₂, N₂, H₂, CO, CO₂, CH₄, C₂H₆, C₂H₄, C₂H₂, C₃H₆ and C₄H₈.
  - Units: µl/l (ppm).
- **Gas-generation patterns:**
  - 150–500 °C: mainly H₂ and CH₄, with less C₂H₄ and C₂H₆.
  - Low-energy discharge: H₂, CH₄ and trace C₂H₂.
  - High-energy discharge (700–1800 °C): a large increase in C₂H₂.

## Failure modes, faults, and operational risks

- **Mechanical faults (detected by SFRA):**
  - Winding deformation, displacement, buckling, twisting and axial deformation
  - Coil collapse, including localized collapse
  - Loosened clamping
  - Short or open circuits
  - Loose core components, core movement and core-joint faults
  - Core grounding damage
  - Faults in the winding-to-bushing (porcelain input) connections
  - On-load tap changer faults, including coking and pitting
- **Electrical and thermal faults (detected by DGA, IEC 60599 classes):**
  - PD: partial discharge
  - D1: low-energy discharge
  - D2: high-energy discharge
  - T1: thermal fault below 300 °C
  - T2: thermal fault 300–700 °C
  - T3: thermal fault above 700 °C
- **Rogers ratio (IEEE C57.104) categories:** no fault, corona PD, high-energy arc, low temperature, below 700 °C, and above 700 °C.
- **Risks:**
  - Undetected mechanical damage can cause dielectric or thermal damage, supply interruptions, and costly repair or replacement.
  - The effect of a given deformation on the SFRA trace varies with transformer design.
- **Limitation noted from prior work:** a transformer turns ratio (TTR) test detects short circuits but cannot identify discharges or internal damage.

## Applicable standards, norms, or industry conventions (when mentioned)

- **IEC 60076-18:** SFRA measurement on power transformers.
- **DL/T 911-2004 (Chinese standard):** Rxy correlation-factor bands and deformation grading. The paper calls it the only standard giving a quantitative comparison of frequency responses.
- **IEEE C57.104:** DGA fault classification and Rogers ratio thresholds.
- **IEC 60599:** DGA fault classes (PD, D1, D2, T1–T3) and ratio limits. The paper sometimes misprints it as "IEC 60559".
  - Single-gas thresholds (ppm): C₂H₂ 1, H₂ 100, CH₄ 120, C₂H₆ 65, CO 350, C₂H₄ 50.
- **ASTM D3612C:** gas chromatography method for dissolved gases in oil.

## Gaps / not supported by this paper

- No online or continuous monitoring. Both methods are periodic, offline or sample-based tests.
- No DGA trending, gas-generation rates or sampling intervals are given.
- No maintenance intervals, costs, work-order workflows or remaining-life estimates are given.
- No thermal monitoring data (top-oil or hot-spot temperature, cooling type) for the cases studied.
- The T2 63 MVA, 220 kV DGA results (Table 13) are truncated and not available.
- Duval triangle procedures are mentioned only via prior work.
- No specific SFRA thresholds are given for the CAP, IND or EESC schemes.
- There are minor data inconsistencies:
  - The Phan Thiet voltage rating differs between the abstract (115/23/11 kV) and the body (110/22 kV).
  - The CO value differs between tables (2131.18 vs 2031.18 ppm).
  - Table 6's heading says it covers temperature-based classes, but it also lists discharge faults.
